1. Introduction

TypeNBash ("TypeNBash," "we," "us," or "our") publishes a multipurpose Integrated Development Environment for macOS (the "Application"). The Application is written in SwiftUI and AppKit for native performance and runs only on macOS. It is distributed directly as a DMG disk image. It supports polyglot projects, including Markdown, LaTeX, software code, and R and Python notebooks for data analysis and research. It includes an integrated console, a WebKit-based preview of localhost applications, optional GitHub integration for source control, or your own optional API provided LLM agents. The Application is designed to be local-first. Your working data resides on your device, in AppStorage and Swift data models, and the Application does not collect personal information on our behalf.

We regard privacy as a core attribute of the product. This Privacy Policy (the "Policy") sets out what personal information we receive (which is limited to what you choose to send us), how we use and disclose it, and what rights and choices are available to you. We have drafted it to be read and understood, not merely to be filed.

Scope. This Policy applies to personal information that we receive through the Application (and its packaged CiteTex extension), our website at https://vdesai.xyz/typenbash/ (the "Website"), and our related support communications (collectively, the "Services"). It does not govern the practices of third parties, including GitHub, or any application you preview through the Application's WebKit component. Those parties are subject to their own policies.

Questions. Privacy inquiries may be directed to vd@vadesai.com.

2. What We Collect & Why

The Application does not collect personal information from you, and it does not transmit your personal information, usage data, or project content to us. We receive only the personal information you voluntarily send us, for example, when you contact us for support. Because we serve users in multiple jurisdictions and are subject to U.S. state privacy laws, we identify below the purpose and legal basis for that limited information. Where we use the term "legal basis," we mean the justification for processing, whether contract, consent, legitimate interest, or legal obligation.

2.1 Information You Provide to Us Directly

Support and correspondence. When you contact us, we receive your email address, the content of your message, and any information you elect to include, so that we may respond and resolve your inquiry. Please do not include sensitive information, such as credentials or private project content, in your message. Legal basis: legitimate interest; consent.

We do not otherwise ask you for personal information in order to use the Application.

2.2 Information Collected Automatically

The Application does not automatically collect or transmit technical, usage, device, or diagnostic information to us. It does not include analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting services that send data to us. Information about how you use the Application remains on your device.

2.3 Your Project Content

The Application is designed so that your project content (source code, documents, notebooks, and similar material) is stored on your device, in AppStorage and Swift data models. It is not transmitted to us in the ordinary operation of the Application. Should you voluntarily transmit content to us, for example in a support request, we shall treat it in accordance with Section 17.

2.4 Information from Third Parties

We do not receive personal information about you from third parties.

User-directed integrations: If you connect a GitHub authorization key, the Application uses it, on your instruction, to communicate with GitHub for source control. As designed, the key is stored on your device and is not sent to us. Similarly, any use of the WebKit preview of localhost applications or your own agents occurs at your direction, and we do not receive the data involved. The data practices of those services are governed by their respective policies.

3. How We Use Your Information

Because the Application does not collect personal information, our use of personal information is limited to what you send us. We use it only for the purposes described below, and we do not process it in a manner incompatible with those purposes without providing notice and, where required, obtaining your consent.

Support and communications (legitimate interest; consent). To read and respond to messages you send us, provide technical support, and resolve your inquiries.

Legal compliance (legal obligation). To comply with applicable legal and regulatory requirements, and to respond to lawful requests.

We do not use personal information for analytics, profiling, advertising, or marketing.

4. When We Share Your Information

We never sell your personal data. We do not "share" personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising, as those terms are defined under the California Consumer Privacy Act, as amended (the "CCPA"), and comparable laws. We do not routinely disclose personal information to third parties. We disclose it only in the circumstances below.

Legal requirements. We may disclose information where we believe in good faith that disclosure is required by law, subpoena, court order, or other lawful process, or is necessary to protect the rights, property, or safety of TypeNBash, our users, or others.

Business transitions. In the event of a merger, acquisition, financing, reorganization, or sale of all or part of our assets, personal information may be transferred to the successor entity. Notwithstanding the foregoing, any such successor shall remain bound by the commitments of this Policy as to previously collected information unless you are given notice and, where required, an opportunity to object.

With your consent or at your direction. We shall disclose personal information for any other purpose only with your consent or at your direction. For example, when you instruct the Application to communicate with GitHub, that communication occurs directly from your device to GitHub.

  2. Correction. You may request that we correct inaccurate or incomplete information.
  3. Deletion. You may request deletion of your personal information, subject to legal exceptions, such as where retention is required by law or to defend legal claims.
  4. Portability. You may request your information in a structured, commonly used, machine-readable format.
  5. Opt out of marketing, sale, sharing, and targeted advertising. We do not engage in the sale or sharing of personal information or in targeted advertising, and we do not currently send marketing. You may still submit an opt-out request at any time.
  6. Withdraw consent. Where processing is based on consent, you may withdraw it at any time. Withdrawal shall not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out before withdrawal.
  7. Restriction and objection. Where applicable, you may request that we restrict processing or object to processing based on legitimate interests.
  8. Non-discrimination. We shall not discriminate against you for exercising any of your rights.

Submit a request to vd@vadesai.com, identifying the right you wish to exercise. We may require information sufficient to verify your identity, and we shall use it solely for that purpose. An authorized agent may submit a request on your behalf, provided that we may require proof of the agent's authority. We shall respond within the period prescribed by applicable law, which is generally 45 days and may be extended once where reasonably necessary, in which case we shall notify you.

5.2 Appeals

If we decline to act on your request, you may appeal by replying to our decision, or by writing to vd@vadesai.com with the subject line "Appeal." We shall inform you in writing of the outcome within the period required by applicable law.

6. Data Security

We implement reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards appropriate to the nature of the personal information we hold and the risks presented, consistent with an enhanced data risk profile. These include limiting access to personal information to persons with a legitimate need for it, and relying upon the infrastructure and security controls of our hosting and payment providers.

The local-first design of the Application is itself a protective measure. By keeping your project content on your device, we reduce the volume of sensitive material that traverses or resides on external systems.

  1. Limitations: No method of transmission or storage is entirely secure. We therefore cannot, and do not, guarantee absolute security.
  2. Your role: You are responsible for the security of your device and credentials. We recommend that you use strong, unique passwords, enable device-level protections available on macOS, keep your operating system and the Application current, and safeguard any GitHub authorization key. If you suspect that a key or account has been compromised, you should revoke the key and notify us promptly.
  3. Breach notification: In the event of a security breach affecting your personal information, we shall notify affected users and relevant regulators as required by applicable law.

7. Data Retention

The Application does not collect personal information, so there is no Application-generated data for us to retain. Project content and settings remain on your device, and you control them. The only personal information we hold is what you send us directly. We retain it only for so long as necessary to fulfill the purposes for which it was received, and thereafter only as required by law.

  1. Support correspondence: Retained for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to and resolve your inquiry and to handle any follow-up, and then deleted upon your request or when no longer needed.
  2. Legal holds and disputes: We may retain information beyond the foregoing period where necessary to establish, exercise, or defend legal claims, or to comply with law.

The rationale is simple. We keep information only while it is needed to help you, and we retain it beyond that point only where the law obliges us to do so.

8. Cookies & Tracking

The Application is a native macOS application and does not rely upon browser cookies for its operation. The Application's WebKit component renders localhost applications that you run. Any cookies those applications set are generated by your own projects, reside locally, and are within your control.

As to the Website, we do not use cookies or similar technologies for cross-context behavioral advertising. To the extent the Website employs cookies, they are limited to those necessary for its operation and security. Should we introduce non-essential cookies or analytics tools, we shall update this Policy and, where required, obtain your consent beforehand. You may manage cookies through the settings of your browser. We do not maintain a separate cookie policy at this time.
